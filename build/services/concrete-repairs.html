Overview

Concrete Repairs for Rental Properties

Cracked sidewalks, deteriorating walkways, and damaged steps are more than an eyesore — they're a liability. Cordova Property Services handles concrete repairs for apartment communities and rental properties: crack filling, spalling repair, staircase restoration, parking lot patching, and pool deck resurfacing. We keep your exterior surfaces safe, functional, and well-maintained.

Why This Matters

Deteriorated concrete creates trip hazards, drives up liability exposure, and signals a poorly maintained property to prospective tenants. Proactive concrete repair costs a fraction of full replacement — and keeps your community looking professional.

Our Process

Simple, Transparent — Start to Finish

1

Assessment & Scope

We inspect all concrete surfaces — walkways, parking areas, steps, pool decks — and document the damage with photos before quoting.

2

Surface Preparation

Damaged sections cleaned, old material removed, and surfaces prepped to ensure proper adhesion and a lasting repair.

3

Repair & Resurfacing

Cracks filled, spalled areas patched, and surfaces resurfaced using commercial-grade materials matched to the existing concrete.

Sealing & Delivery

Repaired areas sealed for durability. Property confirmed ready with documentation of all work completed.
