Overview

Turnkey Services for Rental Properties

At Cordova Property Services, our turnkey service handles every aspect of the unit turn — trash-out, cleaning, painting, repairs, resurfacing, and more — coordinated by a single team. Every vacant day costs you money. We move fast, communicate clearly, and get your unit back on the market faster.

Why This Matters

Juggling multiple vendors slows everything down. One team that handles the full turn cuts your timeline significantly, reduces back-and-forth, and ensures every service is completed to a consistent standard.
