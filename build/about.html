Who We Are

Your All-in-One Partner for Apartment Services

Property managers shouldn’t have to juggle multiple vendors to turn one unit.

Cordova Property Services is a full-service maintenance company specializing in apartment communities, proudly serving properties across Pensacola, Gulf Breeze, Navarre, Fort Walton, Crestview, Milton, Daphne, Mobile, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach, and Foley.

We partner with property management companies and owners to deliver fast, dependable, and high-quality solutions tailored to the day-to-day needs of multifamily operations.

Our mission: service should be simple, responsive, and solution-driven. We understand the urgency of unit turns and maintenance timelines.
